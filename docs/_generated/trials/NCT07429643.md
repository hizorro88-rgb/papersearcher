## 시험 개요

this is a randomized controlled trial aims to compare between patients with locally advanced pancreatic cancer treated with EUS-guided injection of gemcitabine in addition to systemic chemotherapy, versus those who treated with the standard systemic chemotherapy alone, regarding to(progression -free survival and response rate). ??? note "선정/제외 기준 원문 (영어)"

    Inclusion Criteria:
    
    * Age \> 18 years old.
    * Patients recently diagnosed to have pancreatic adenocarcinoma based on histopathological examination.
    * Locally advanced potentially resectable pancreatic cancer . .Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group performance status of 0 to 1. .Signed informed consent.
    
    Exclusion Criteria:
    
    * Age \< 18 years old.
    * Metastatic pancreatic cancer.
    * prior pancreatic surgery or chemotherapy or radiotherapy. concurrent or prior malignancy. coexistent medical condition precluding protocol therapy.
