## 시험 개요

The goal of this clinical trial is to learn whether VS-7375, an investigational drug taken by mouth that blocks a protein called KRAS G12D that helps cancer cells grow, is safe and works better when combined with other cancer treatments than standard chemotherapy alone. The study will include people with pancreatic cancer that has spread to other parts of the body and has a specific genetic change called KRAS G12D.

??? note "선정/제외 기준 원문 (영어)"

    Inclusion Criteria:
    
    * Histologic or cytologic confirmation of metastatic PDAC
    * Measurable disease according to RECIST v1.1
    * Treatment-naïve for metastatic disease
    * Local testing confirmed KRAS G12D mutation (tissue required for confirmatory central testing)
    * (ECOG PS) of 0 or 1.
    * Adequate organ function
    
    Exclusion Criteria:
    
    * Underwent major surgical procedure within 4 weeks prior to Cycle 1 Day 1
    * Received \> 1 month of systemic chemotherapy for the treatment of metastatic disease.
    * Receipt of chemotherapy, targeted anticancer therapy, or radiotherapy within 4 weeks or 5 half-lives
    * Have any documented co-existing pathogenic or likely pathogenic RAS mutation(s) in KRAS
    * Prior treatment with any agent that directly or indirectly targets RAS
    * Tumors that are locally confirmed Microsatellite Instability-High (MSI-H) or deficient Mismatch Repair (dMMR).
    * Symptomatic, untreated, or actively progressing known central nervous system (CNS) metastases.
    * Inability to swallow oral medications
    * Current need of supplemental O2 90%.
    * Receipt of an allogeneic tissue/solid organ or hematopoietic transplant
    * History or current diagnosis of interstitial lung disease (ILD) or pneumonitis
    * Other protocol defined inclusion/exclusion criteria may apply
