## 시험 개요

이 연구는 MTAP 유전자 결손이 있는 절제 가능 또는 경계성 절제 가능 췌장암 환자를 대상으로 합니다. 수술 전후에 navlimetostat(나블리메토스타트, BMS-986504)와 변형 FOLFIRINOX(mFOLFIRINOX)를 함께 투여하는 병용요법을 평가합니다. 연구 목적은 해당 병용요법의 안전성, 수행 가능성 및 권장 용량을 확인하는 것입니다. 총 18명의 환자를 대상으로 진행되는 1b/2상 임상시험입니다.

- MTAP 유전자 결손이 확인된 절제 가능 및 경계성 절제 가능 췌장암 환자 18명을 대상으로 합니다.
- 수술 전후 치료로서 navlimetostat(BMS-986504)과 mFOLFIRINOX 병용요법의 안전성과 권장 용량을 평가합니다.
- 이전 전신 항암 치료 경험이 없는 환자를 대상으로 연구가 진행됩니다.

## 이 시험이 맞는 환자 (AI 정리, 원문 확인 필요)

**이전 항암치료 경험이 없고 MTAP 결손이 확인된 절제 가능 또는 경계성 절제 가능 췌장암 환자**

| 항목 | 조건 |
|---|---|
| 질병 상태 | 수술 가능·경계성, 수술 후 보조치료, 수술 전 선행치료 |
| 이전 항암 치료 | 1차 치료(아직 항암 안 받은 분) |
| 받았으면 참여 불가 | 젬시타빈, FOLFIRINOX, 백금계(옥살리플라틴 등), 5-FU 계열, 이리노테칸, 탁산(아브락산 등), 면역항암제, KRAS 억제제, 방사선치료 |
| 필요한 유전자·표지자 | 기타 표적 |
| ECOG 수행능력 | 1 이하 |

주요 제외 기준: 이전 췌장암 치료 경험(navlimetostat 시작 전 mFOLFIRINOX 최대 2회 투여 제외) · 원발 암 부위에 대한 방사선 치료 병력 또는 계획 · 알약 복용 불가능 또는 유의미한 위장관 질환 · 최근 6개월 이내 심근경색 또는 불안정 형통 등 심혈관 질환 · 활동성 B형/C형 간염, HIV 감염 또는 중증 만성 감염

## 참여 조건 (AI 정리, 원문 확인 필요)

선정 기준:
- 만 18세 이상
- 조직학적 또는 세포학적으로 확진된 췌장관선암
- 차세대 염기서열 분석(NGS)을 통해 암 조직에서 MTAP 결손(deletion 또는 loss)이 확인된 환자
- 원격 전이가 없으며, 절제 가능 또는 경계성 절제 가능 단계이고 이전 치료 경험이 없는 환자
- ECOG 수행능력 평가 점수 0점 또는 1점
- mFOLFIRINOX 투여 및 완치 목적의 췌장 절제술을 받을 수 있는 상태

제외 기준:
- navlimetostat 시작 전 mFOLFIRINOX 최대 2회 투여 외에 췌장암에 대한 이전 치료 경험이 있는 경우
- 원발 부위에 대한 수술 전 방사선 치료를 받았거나 수술 후 방사선 치료가 계획된 경우
- 알약을 삼킬 수 없거나 약물 흡수를 방해하는 위장관 질환이 있는 경우
- 등록 전 6개월 이내 심근경색, 불안정 형통, 심장 스텐트 시술, 심부전 등 주요 심혈관 질환 병력이 있는 경우
- HIV 감염, 활성 B형 또는 C형 간염, 기타 치료가 필요한 중증 만성 감염이 있는 경우

??? note "선정/제외 기준 원문 (영어)"

    Inclusion Criteria:
    
    1. Age ≥ 18 years.
    2. Histologically and/or cytologically confirmed pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma.
    3. MTAP deletion or loss in tumor tissue as diagnosed by next generation sequencing.
    4. Treatment naïve and clinically staged as resectable or borderline resectable, without evidence of distant metastatic disease.
    5. Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group (ECOG) performance status of 0 or 1.
    6. Eligible to receive mFOLFIRINOX and undergo curative-intent pancreatic resection.
    7. Females are eligible to participate if they are not pregnant or breastfeeding. If childbearing potential must have a negative serum or urine pregnancy test at screening. IOCBP are not permitted to use hormonal contraceptive methods alone as a highly effective method of contraception and must use an additional non-hormonal highly effective method of contraception (as described in Appendix B).
    8. WOCBP are not permitted to use hormonal contraceptive methods alone as a highly effective method of contraception and must use an additional non-hormonal highly effective method of contraception (as described in Appendix B).
    9. A female (as assigned at birth) is eligible to participate if they are not pregnant or breastfeeding and at least 1 of the following conditions applies:
    
       1. Is not an IOCBP, OR
       2. Is an IOCBP and using a combination of a hormonal and a non-hormonal contraceptive method or a non-hormonal method alone that is highly effective (with a failure rate of \< 1% per year), as described in Appendix B, during the intervention period and for at least 9 months after the last dose of study intervention, or according to approved local product label requirements for individual agents, whichever is longer. IOCBP participants must also agree not to donate eggs (ova, oocytes) for the purpose of reproduction for the same period.
    10. Females of child-bearing potential and males with partners of child-bearing potential must agree not to donate eggs (ova, oocytes) for the purpose of reproduction or sperm and to practice sexual abstinence or use the forms of contraception listed in Child-Bearing Potential/Pregnancy section for the duration of study participation and for 9 months after the last dose of the study medication.
    11. If needed, male (as assigned at birth) participants should be advised to seek advice about sperm donation and cryopreservation of germ cells before treatment.
    12. Breastfeeding partners of male (as assigned at birth) participants should be advised to consult their health care provider about using appropriate highly effective contraception during the time the male participant is required to use condoms.
    13. Capable of understanding and complying with the protocol requirements and has signed the informed consent document.
    14. Adequate organ and marrow function as defined below:
    
        1. Absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1,500/μl
        2. Platelets ≥ 100,000/μl
        3. Hemoglobin \> 9 g/dL
        4. AST/ALT ≤ 3 X upper limit of normal (ULN)
        5. Total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 X upper limit of normal (ULN)
        6. Calculated CrCL ≥50 mL/min using the Cockcroft-Gault formula.
        7. INR or PT ≤ 1.5× ULN; if receiving anticoagulant therapy, PT or aPTT should be within therapeutic range.
    15. Provide written, informed consent to participate in the study and follow the study procedures.
    16. Willing to provide mandatory tissue and blood samples for research purposes.
    
    Exclusion Criteria:
    
    1. Prior treatment for pancreatic adenocarcinoma other than up to two doses of mFOLFIRINOX administered prior to initiation of navlimetostat.
    2. Concurrent severe and/or uncontrolled medical conditions which may compromise participation in the study.
    3. Female patients who are pregnant or breast feeding.
    4. Patients of either sex, not willing to use an effective method of birth control.
    5. Live vaccines within 30 days of planned start of study medication, during treatment and for 90 days after treatment. Examples of live vaccines include, but are not limited to, the following: measles, mumps, rubella, varicella/zoster, yellow fever, rabies, BCG, and typhoid vaccine.
    6. Patients taking medications specified by the protocol as prohibited for administration in combination with study drugs.
    7. Radiotherapy to the primary tumor prior to or planned post-surgery.
    8. Inability to swallow pills or any significant gastrointestinal disease which would preclude the adequate oral absorption of medications.
    9. Major surgical procedure, open biopsy, or significant traumatic injury within 28 days prior to the start of study or anticipation of need for major surgical procedure during the course of the study other than surgical resection of the pancreatic tumor.
    10. Serious, non-healing wound, ulcer, or bone fracture.
    11. History of myocardial infarction, unstable angina, cardiac or other vascular stenting, angioplasty, or surgery within 6 months prior to study enrollment.
    12. Cardiac abnormalities including:
    
        1. LVEF \< 50%
        2. QTcF prolongation \> 480 msec, except for right bundle branch block, per the investigator's assessment.
    13. Uncontrolled/symptomatic or significant cardiovascular conditions within 6 months prior to randomization, including, but not limited to, any of the following: cardiac angioplasty or stenting, unstable angina pectoris, myocardial infarction, stroke/transient ischemic attack, coronary artery bypass graft surgery, symptomatic peripheral vascular disease, New York Heart Association class III-IV congestive heart failure, pericarditis, myocarditis, atrial fibrillation, or other arrhythmias (eg, ventricular tachycardia, ventricular fibrillation, or Torsades de pointes).
    14. Ongoing need for a medication with a known risk of Torsades de Pointes that cannot be switched to alternative treatment prior to study entry.
    15. HIV seropositivity, active hepatitis B or C infection, or other serious chronic infection requiring ongoing treatment.
    16. Known dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase (DPD) deficiency
    17. Unable or unwilling to abide by the study protocol or cooperate fully with the investigator or designee.
    18. Known chronic liver disease, autoimmune hepatitis, or sclerosing cholangitis.
    19. Receiving any other agent which would be considered as a treatment for the primary neoplasm.
    20. Treatment with another investigational device or study drug or participation in another drug study within 30 days of screening. Other investigational procedures while participating in this study are excluded.
    21. Known hypersensitivity to the active substances or to any of the excipients.
    22. Patients with another malignancy for which treatment is required, per investigator's clinical judgment.
