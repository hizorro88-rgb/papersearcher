## 시험 개요

본 연구는 소마토스타틴 유사체(SSA) 치료 중 진행을 보인 1~2등급 중장, 후장 또는 췌장 신경내분비종양(NET) 환자를 대상으로 합니다. 이 환자들이 방사성수용체 표적치료(PRRT)를 시작할 때 SSA 투여를 지속하는 것과 중단하는 것의 효과를 비교합니다. 주요 목표는 PRRT 시작 후 20개월 무진행 생존율(PFS)과 연구 순조성(등록률 및 환자 수용도)을 평가하는 것입니다. 임상시험은 무작위 배정, 비교 대조가 없는 공개 다기관 2상 임상시험으로 진행됩니다.

- 대상 질환은 1~2등급의 중장, 후장 또는 췌장 신경내분비종양(NET)입니다.
- 목표 인원은 총 78명이며, PRRT 시작 전 SSA 지속 군과 중단 군으로 무작위 배정됩니다.
- 주요 평가지표는 PRRT 시작 후 20개월 무진행 생존율(PFS) 및 치료 타당성(등록률과 환자 수용도)입니다.

## 참여 조건 (AI 정리, 원문 확인 필요)

선정 기준:
- 18세 이상이며, 수술 불가하고 전이성이며 소마토스타틴 유사체(SSA) 치료 중 진행하여 PRRT가 필요한 잘 분화된 중장, 후장 또는 췌장 신경내분비종양 환자
- RECIST 1.1 기준에 따른 측정 가능 병변 존재
- WHO 1등급 또는 2등급 (Ki67 ≤20% 및 유사분열 계수 ≤20/HPF)
- 비기능성 신경내분비종양 (카르시노이드 증후군 증상 조절용으로 용량 증량이나 단기 SSA가 필요하지 않았던 경우 등)
- 연구 등록 전 최소 12주 동안 성장 조절 용량의 SSA 투여를 받은 환자
- PRRT에 적합한 소마토스타틴 수용체 발현을 보이는 SSTR PET 스캔 결과
- ECOG 수행능력 0~2

제외 기준:
- 위 및 폐 신경내분비종양 환자
- 표준 성장 조절 용량 미만의 SSA를 투여 중인 환자
- 이전 화학요법 또는 표적치료(예: 에베로리무스)를 받은 적이 있는 환자
- 이전에 PRRT 치료를 받은 적이 있는 환자
- 조절되지 않는 중추신경계 전이 환자
- 임신부 또는 수유부

??? note "선정/제외 기준 원문 (영어)"

    Inclusion Criteria:
    
    1. Adults over 18 years of age with well or moderately differentiated mid or hindgut neuroendocrine tumour, or pancreatic neuroendocrine tumour; metastatic and inoperable, demonstrating progression despite SSA treatment of sufficient disease magnitude to warrant PRRT as determined by the treating clinician and/or the NET Multidisciplinary Team (MDT). See section 4.1 regarding the recording of the reason(s).
    2. Must have measurable disease on triphasic CT/MRI as per RECIST 1.1.
    3. Ki67 ≤20% AND mitotic count ≤20 per HPF (i.e., WHO grade 1 or 2).
    
       • Note: Participants are eligible if only a single proliferation index has been performed due to local institutional practice, provided that the results are consistent with WHO grade 1 or 2 NET. However, if the results for both Ki67 and mitotic count are available, both results will need to be reported and are subject to eligibility assessment.
    4. Non-functioning NET: SSA treatment will have been commenced for control of tumour growth and not for carcinoid or other hormone overproduction syndrome, as judged by the clinician and/or NET MDT. Non-functioning NET is judged by the treating clinical team based on patient symptomatology.
    
       In addition, for this study, non-functioning tumour is defined as:
       * Never had escalation of the SSA treatment dose to control carcinoid or other hormone-related symptoms.
       * Never required short acting SSA treatment to control carcinoid or other hormone-related symptoms.
       * For mid and hind gut (GI cohort) patients:
    
         * 24-hour urine 5-hydroxyindoleacetic acid (5HIAA) \<3x upper limit of normal.
         * No significant carcinoid induced valvular heart disease. I.e. Echocardiogram to be within 12 weeks of study randomisation, and deemed safe to proceed with PRRT by the treating team.
       * Note: routine measurement of gastrin, insulin, C-peptide levels, glucagon etc. is not required unless clinically indicated.
    5. Patient has been receiving growth-controlling doses of SSA for at least 12 weeks prior to study enrolment, for example a minimum of 30mg Octreotide or 120mg Lanreotide monthly. However, other SSA formulations used at growth-controlling doses are also eligible.
    6. Uptake on SSTR PET scan demonstrating somatostatin receptor expression that is suitable for PRRT as judged by the clinical team. FDG PET scans are to be done at the judgement of the treating team and are not required for enrolment into this study.
    7. PRRT is deemed the most appropriate next treatment step (i.e., patient is inoperable, and liver directed therapies are not preferred).
    8. ECOG performance status 0-2.
    9. Written informed consent. Patients must be willing to either cease or continue SSA, depending on which study arm they are randomised to. Patients must be willing to comply with all other study requirements.
    10. Adequate renal, hepatic and haematologic function as judged by the treating team.
    11. Life expectancy of at least 12 months.
    
    Exclusion Criteria:
    
    1. This study is for pancreatic, mid-gut and hind-gut NET only. Gastric and lung NETs are excluded.
    2. Any patient on an SSA dose lower than the standard growth-control dose. Patients must have been on an SSA equivalent to Octreotide 30mg or Lanreotide 120mg for at least 3 months prior to study enrolment.
    3. Prior chemotherapy or targeted therapy (e.g., everolimus). Patients who have received prior local therapy, including external beam radiotherapy and liver directed therapy prior to or during SSA therapy are eligible.
    4. Any contraindication to PRRT, as per local institutional practice.
    5. Pregnancy. For female patients of childbearing potential and male patients with a female partner who is of childbearing potential, contraception and counselling is required.
    6. Prior PRRT. Patients being considered for re-treatment with PRRT are not eligible.
    7. Uncontrolled central nervous system metastases. Patients must have completed any surgery or radiation at least 4 weeks prior to enrolment and must be off corticosteroids for at least 2 weeks.
    8. Any patient, in the opinion of the investigator, who will not comply with study assessments and follow up visits. These might include any social, psychological, or geographical concerns, including alcohol/drug abuse.
    9. Any poorly controlled concurrent medical illness that may prevent the patient from complying with study assessments and follow up. This is to be judged by the treating team.
    10. Any concurrent or prior malignancy that, in the opinion of the treating team, may interfere with study assessments and endpoints.
