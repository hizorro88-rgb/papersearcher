## 시험 개요

이 임상시험은 수술이 불가능하거나 올리고전이(oligometastatic)가 있는 1차 치료 단계의 췌관선암(pancreatic adenocarcinoma) 환자를 대상으로 합니다. 대조군은 젬시타빈(gemcitabine)과 nab-paclitaxel(냅-파클리탁셀) 병용 치료만 받고, 실험군은 여기에 변조 전기고주파온열치료(modulated electro-hyperthermia, mEHT)를 병행합니다. 치료는 28일을 1주기로 하여 진행되며 4주 차는 휴식기로 구성됩니다. 현재 이 임상시험은 철회(withdrawn)되었습니다.

- 수술 불가능 및 올리고전이 췌장암 1차 치료 환자를 대상으로 한 1:1 무작위 배정 오픈라벨 연구입니다.
- 실험군은 젬시타빈(gemcitabine 1000 mg/m2) 및 nab-paclitaxel(125 mg/m2)과 함께 주 3회 전기고주파온열치료(mEHT)를 받습니다.
- 대조군은 젬시타빈과 nab-paclitaxel 항암화학요법 단독 치료를 받습니다.
- 이 임상시험은 현재 철회(withdrawn)되어 환자를 모집하지 않습니다.

## 참여 조건 (AI 정리, 원문 확인 필요)

선정 기준:
- 만 18세 이상
- 기대 여명 6개월 이상
- 조직학적 또는 세포학적으로 새로 진단된 췌장암
- 전신 치료가 필요한 진행성 병기(3기 및 4기)
- ECOG 수행능력 상태 0~2
- gemcitabine + nab-paclitaxel 항암화학요법 치료에 적합하고 연구자에 의해 지정된 자
- 임상시험 참여 의사가 있고 동의서에 서명한 자

제외 기준:
- 만 18세 이하
- 다량의 복수(major ascites) 존재
- 이전에 항암 치료를 받은 적이 있는 자
- 선정 5년 이내에 치료가 필요한 다른 암 병력(자궁경부 상피내암, 비흑색종 피부암 제외)
- 중증 신경병증 또는 당뇨병성 신경병증 존재
- 임상적으로 유의한 혈액학적, 간장 또는 신장 기능 장애
- 조절되지 않는 심혈관 질환, 고혈압, 빈맥 또는 심각한 심장 부정맥
- 활동성 감염 또는 중증의 기저 질환(만성 간염, HIV, 치료되지 않은 갑상선 질환, 자가면역 질환 등)
- 해부학적 부위에 이식된 활성 의료기기(심박조율기 등) 존재
- 임신 중이거나 모유 수유 중인 환자

??? note "선정/제외 기준 원문 (영어)"

    Inclusion Criteria:
    
    1. At least 18 years of age
    2. Life expectancy ≥ 6 months
    3. De novo histological/cytological diagnosis of pancreatic cancer
    4. Advanced stage disease (stage III and IV) requiring systemic treatment - according to the following criteria:
    5. ECOG status: 0-2
    6. Suitable for and designated by the investigator for chemotherapy with gemcintabine + nab-paclitaxel
    7. Willingness to participate in the trial and signed the informed consent form for the protocol
    
    Exclusion Criteria:
    
    1. Patient is ≤ 18 years of age.
    2. Presence of major ascites
    3. Has already received some anticancer therapy
    4. Any previous cancer requiring anti-tumor treatment within 5 years prior to selection, except: in situ cervical or uterine cancer and non-melanoma skin cancer.
    5. Co-existing serious diseases:
    
       1. Presence of severe neuropathy requiring medical treatment, diabetic neuropathy.
       2. Clinically significant hematological, hepatic or renal dysfunction, as defined below:
    
          * Neutrophil count \< 1.5 G/L and platelet count \< 100 G/L
          * bilirubin \> 1.5 times the upper limit of normal range (ULN), except for known Gilbert's disease
          * AST and/or ALT \> 2.5 times the upper limit of the normal range
          * Serum creatinine \> 1.5 times the upper limit of the normal range.
       3. Clinically significant cardiovascular disease in the medical history, unless the disease is adequately controlled. E.g. New York Heart Association (NYHA) Class II or worse congestive heart failure (moderate limitation of physical activity; well-being at rest but normal activity is associated with fatigue, rapid heart rate or dyspnoea).
       4. Uncontrolled hypertension with resting systolic ≥ 180 mmHg, resting diastolic ≥ 110 mmHg.
       5. Resting sinus tachycardia with a pulse ≥ 110/min.
       6. History of sympathetic or treatment-naive cardiac arrhythmia. Atrial fibrillation or flutter controlled with medication is not an exclusion for participation in the study.
       7. Major cardiovascular event (e.g. myocardial infarction, unstable angina, cerebral vascular accident (CVA), etc.) in the 6 months prior to randomisation.
       8. Active infection or severe underlying disease that renders the patient unfit for treatment according to the study protocol.
    
          * A current diagnosis of chronic hepatitis, Hepatitis B surface antigen positive, Hepatitis C antibody positive and/or other clinically active liver disease requiring treatment.
          * Known HIV infection.
          * Untreated thyroid disease.
          * Systemic autoimmune disease.
       9. Any psychiatric condition in the medical history that may result in the patient being unable to understand or comply with the requirements of the study, having reduced communication skills or being unable to give informed consent.
    6. Any active medical device implanted in the anatomical area, such as pacemakers.
    7. Known severe hypersensitivity to any of the chemotherapies used in the study.
    8. Pregnancy or breast-feeding (patients of childbearing potential must use effective contraception throughout the study and for 3 months after the end of treatment). The method of effective contraception is at the discretion of the investigator.
    9. History of drug or alcohol dependence within 6 months prior to screening.
    10. Unable to comply with the study plan for medical, psychological, family, geographical or other reasons.
    11. Institutionalisation by administrative or judicial decision.
