## 시험 개요

이 연구는 국소진행성 췌장암 환자를 대상으로 비가역적 전기소작술(IRE)과 함께 시험약인 IP-001 주사제의 안전성, 내인성, 예비 효과를 평가하기 위한 1상 임상시험입니다. 대상은 3기 국소진행성 췌장선암(LAPC) 환자 10명입니다. 환자들은 IRE 절제술 후 종양 내에 IP-001을 투여받습니다. 초록에 효과 및 생존기간 등의 수치는 명시되지 않았습니다.

- 목표 환자 수는 10명입니다.
- 3기 국소진행성 췌장선암 환자를 대상으로 합니다.
- 중재 방법으로 1.0% IP-001 주사제와 비가역적 전기소작술(IRE)을 사용합니다.
- 효과 및 생존 기간 관련 수호는 초록에 명시되지 않았습니다.

## 참여 조건 (AI 정리, 원문 확인 필요)

선정 기준:
* 동의서 서명 시점 연령 만 18세 이상
* NCCN 가이드라인에 따른 임상 3기 췌장암 진단
* NCCN 가이드라인에 따른 표준 선행/유도 화학요법을 최소 2개월 이상 받았고 질병이 안정적이거나 반응을 보였으며 연구 치료 전 최소 21일의 휴약기를 거친 경우
* 3상 CT를 통해 측정 가능한 췌장 종양이 있는 경우 (또는 수술 중 초음파 사용 가능)
* 비가역적 전기소작술 대상자에 해당
* 수술을 견딜 수 있는 경우
* 사구체 여과율 > 30 mL/min/1.73 m2
* ECOG 수행능력 0 또는 1

제조 기준:
* 조개, 게, 갑각류 또는 임상 시험 구성 요소에 알려진 알레르기 반응이 있는 경우
* 스크리닝 시점에 다른 췌장암 임상시험에 참여 중인 경우
* 뉴욕심장협회(NYHA) 3기 또는 4기 심장 질환
* 연구 참여 전 조절되지 않는 감염이 있는 경우
* 등록 전 3개월 이내에 심근경색이 있었던 경우

??? note "선정/제외 기준 원문 (영어)"

    Inclusion Criteria:
    
    * Age ≥ 18 years at time of signing Informed consent Form
    * Diagnosed with clinical stage III pancreatic cancer (per National Comprehensive Cancer Network \[NCCN\] guidelines)
    * Has undergone standard of care (per NCCN guidelines) neoadjuvant/induction base chemotherapy for at least 2 months in duration and demonstrated stable or responsive disease, and completed a wash-out period of at least 21 days prior to study treatment
    * Pancreatic tumor is measurable (bi-dimensional tumor measurements per 3-phase CT scan). If preoperative CT cannot provide 2-dimensional size criteria, then intra-operative ultrasound will be used for baseline size criteria
    * Is a candidate for irreversible electroporation
    * Able to tolerate surgery
    * Glomerular filtration rate \> 30 mL/min/1.73 m2
    * Aspartate aminotransferase/Alanine aminotransferase \< 3 times the upper limit of normal (ULN) and total bilirubin \< 1.5 ULN
    * Absolute neutrophil count ≥1.5 × 10⁹/L, platelet count ≥ 100 × 10⁹/L, and hemoglobin ≥ 9 g/dL
    * Stable surgical post-operative course as defined by the operative surgeon (Eastern Cooperative Oncology Group \[ECOG\]) of 0 or 1
    * Women of childbearing potential
    
      1. Must have a negative serum human chorionic gonadotropin pregnancy test prior to irreversible electroporation
      2. Are using highly effective contraception, are not lactating, and agree not to become pregnant prior to and during the trial treatment period and for 187 days after treatment with the investigational drug
    * Men agree not to donate sperm or to father a child during the trial treatment period and for 97 days after treatment with the investigational drug
    * Participants on therapeutic anticoagulation are eligible if therapy can be safely held or bridged to achieve an INR \< 1.5 per institutional guidelines
    * Adverse events from prior therapy must be resolved to Grade ≤ 1 severity.
    * Able to comprehend and have signed the Informed Consent Form to participate in the study
    
    Exclusion Criteria:
    
    * Known allergic reaction to shellfish, crabs, crustaceans, or any trial components
    * Participating in another clinical trial for the treatment of pancreatic cancer at the time of screening
    * Are pregnant or currently breastfeeding
    * New York Heart Association Class III or IV heart disease
    * Inability to discontinue medications known to prolong the QT interval or induce Torsades de Pointes before study entry, with drug-specific discontinuation timing documented
    * Uncontrolled infection requiring antibiotic therapy
    * Contraindication to heparin
    * Major surgery within 14 days of enrollment
    * Prior high dose radiation therapy defined as total dose of \> 5400 cGy
    * Have a cardiac pacemaker or ICD implanted that cannot be deactivated during IRE procedure
    * Clinically significant history of epilepsy or cardiac arrhythmia
    * Have nonremovable implants with metal parts within 1cm of the target lesion
    * Had a myocardial infarction within 3 months prior to enrollment (informed consent date)
    * Actively bleeding, anticoagulated, or have a known or suspected coagulopathy which has not been corrected
    * Autoimmune disease and/or medical condition requiring systemic treatment with corticosteroids that cannot be stopped during treatment
