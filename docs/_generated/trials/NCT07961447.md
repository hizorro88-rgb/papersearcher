## 시험 개요

This is a prospective, multicenter, randomized, open-label, phase II clinical trial evaluating the efficacy and safety of a triplet regimen combining targeted therapy (nimotuzumab), immunotherapy (serplulimab), and chemotherapy (GnP regimen: gemcitabine plus nab-paclitaxel) versus GnP regimen alone as first-line treatment for patients with locally advanced pancreatic adenocarcinoma.

??? note "선정/제외 기준 원문 (영어)"

    Inclusion Criteria:
    
    * 1\. Age 18 to 75 years, male or female.
    * 2\. Histologically confirmed pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma, judged as locally advanced pancreatic cancer (LAPC) by a multidisciplinary team (MDT) based on pathology and imaging (CT/MRI, and FDG-PET-CT if necessary), referencing NCCN guidelines.
    * 3\. No prior anti-tumor therapy (including chemotherapy, radiotherapy, surgery, radiofrequency ablation, irreversible electroporation, or other investigational therapies).
    * 4\. ECOG performance status of 0 or 1.
    * 5\. At least one measurable primary tumor lesion per RECIST v1.1 criteria (spiral CT long diameter \>= 10 mm, or lymph node short diameter \>= 15 mm).
    * 6\. Adequate major organ function within 14 days prior to enrollment: Bone marrow (WBC \>= 3.5x10\^9/L, ANC \>= 1.5x10\^9/L, PLT \>= 80x10\^9/L, Hb \>= 90 g/L); Liver (ALT/AST \<= 3x ULN, TBIL \<= 1.5x ULN); Kidney (Cr \<= 1.5x ULN, GFR \>= 60 ml/min/1.73m2); Coagulation (PT, APTT, INR \<= 1.5x ULN); Electrolytes (\<= Grade 1 per NCI-CTCAE v6.0); QTc interval \<= 480 ms.
    * 7\. No history of autoimmune disease and no current active autoimmune disease.
    * 8\. Life expectancy of at least 3 months.
    * 9\. Provision of written informed consent prior to any study-related procedures; able to understand and comply with the study protocol.
    
    Exclusion Criteria:
    
    * 1\. Severe underlying diseases or conditions judged by surgeons as unsuitable for surgery, including but not limited to: active infection requiring systemic therapy; uncontrolled diabetes or hypertension; decompensated heart failure, unstable angina, or acute myocardial infarction within 3 months; malignant ascites or pleural effusion; severe portal hypertension or cavernous transformation of the portal vein; gastrointestinal obstruction; respiratory insufficiency (requiring oxygen); severe pulmonary disease; CNS or psychiatric disorders.
    * 2\. Lymph node metastasis beyond the standard radical pancreatectomy range as judged by surgeons or MDT.
    * 3\. Tumor judged as resectable, borderline resectable, or metastatic pancreatic cancer by imaging or MDT.
    * 4\. Known allergy to any study drug or its excipients.
    * 5\. History of other malignancies within 5 years (except adequately treated basal cell carcinoma of the skin or carcinoma in situ of the cervix).
    * 6\. Requirement for concurrent anti-tumor therapies other than the study protocol (e.g., chemotherapy, targeted therapy, hormone therapy, immunotherapy, radiotherapy, anti-tumor traditional Chinese medicine).
    * 7\. Prior or current treatment with anti-PD-1/PD-L1/CTLA-4 immunotherapy.
    * 8\. Prior treatment with EGFR monoclonal antibodies or EGFR-TKIs.
    * 9\. Immunodeficiency or receiving chronic systemic steroid therapy (doses \> 10 mg/day prednisone equivalent) or any other form of immunosuppressive therapy within 7 days prior to the first dose.
    * 10\. Administration of live vaccines within 30 days prior to the first dose.
    * 11\. Major surgery within 28 days prior to enrollment or planned major surgery during the study.
    * 12\. Uncontrolled third-space fluid accumulation (e.g., massive pleural effusion or ascites).
    * 13\. History of gastrointestinal perforation or suspected risk of perforation.
    * 14\. Concurrent use of strong CYP3A4 inhibitors or inducers.
    * 15\. Known HIV positive, active Hepatitis B (HBsAg positive and HBV DNA \>= 500 IU/mL), or active Hepatitis C (HCV antibody positive and HCV RNA positive).
    * 16\. History of or current interstitial lung disease.
    * 17\. Prior solid organ transplantation or allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation.
    * 18\. Known or suspected central nervous system metastases.
    * 19\. Women who are pregnant, planning to become pregnant, or breastfeeding.
    * 20\. Refusal to use effective contraception during the study and for 6 months after the last dose.
    * 21\. Participation in another clinical trial of drugs or medical devices within 4 weeks prior to enrollment.
    * 22\. Other conditions deemed unsuitable for enrollment by the investigator.
