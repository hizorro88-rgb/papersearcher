## 시험 개요

이 임상시험은 진행성 고형암 성인 환자를 대상으로 MC001의 안전성과 내인성을 평가하고 적정 용량을 결정하기 위해 진행됩니다. 특히 췌장관선암(PDAC)과 비인암(nasopharyngeal carcinoma) 환자를 대상으로 확장 코호트를 운영합니다. 연구 참가자들은 질병이 진행되거나 허용할 수 없는 독성이 나타날 때까지 21일마다 MC001을 투여받습니다. 본 연구는 최초로 사람을 대상으로 하는 공개(open-label), 제1상(Phase I) 임상시험입니다.

- 목표 인원은 최대 80명입니다.
- 치료 주기는 21일(Q3W)이며 정맥 주사로 투여됩니다.
- 초기 용량 돋움 단계 이후 췌장관선암(PDAC) 등 대상 질환에 대한 확장 코호트가 진행됩니다.

## 이 시험이 맞는 환자 (AI 정리, 원문 확인 필요)

**표준 치료에 불응하거나 치료가 없는 FRα 발현 진행성 고형암 및 췌장관선암(PDAC) 환자**

| 항목 | 조건 |
|---|---|
| 질병 상태 | 전이성(4기), 국소진행(수술 불가) |
| 이전 항암 치료 | 이전 항암 1가지 이상 받은 분 |
| 필요한 유전자·표지자 | 기타 표적 |
| ECOG 수행능력 | 1 이하 |
| 측정 가능 병변 | 필요 |

주요 제외 기준: MMAE 함유 ADC 약물 투여 경력 · 2등급 이상의 말초 신경병증 · 활동성 중추신경계 전이 · 조절되지 않는 활성 감염 · 치료가 필요한 두 번째 활성 악성종양

## 참여 조건 (AI 정리, 원문 확인 필요)

선정 기준:
* 만 21세 이상인 환자
* 표준 치료가 없거나 불가능한 진행성 또는 전이성 고형암
* 용량 확장의 경우 50% 이상의 종양세포에서 중등도 이상의 막성 FRα 발현
* RECIST version 1.1에 따른 측정 가능한 병변 존재
* ECOG 수행능력 상태 0 또는 1
* 적절한 장기 및 골수 기능 유지

제외 기준:
* MMAE 함유 항체-약물 복합체(ADC)에 대한 이전 노출력 또는 과민반응
* 기저 시점에서 2등급 이상의 말초 신경병증
* 치료되지 않은 활성 중추신경계(CNS) 전이 또는 수막염
* 임상적으로 유의미한 안구 질환
* 조절되지 않는 동반 질환 또는 활성 감염

??? note "선정/제외 기준 원문 (영어)"

    Inclusion Criteria:
    
    1. Age ≥21 years at the time of informed consent.
    2. Histology or cytology confirmation of advanced or metastatic solid tumour that is unresectable or refractory to standard therapy, or for which no standard therapy is available.
    3. Tumour FRα expression, defined as:
    
       * Dose escalation: No predefined FRα expression level required for inclusion into dose escalation.
       * Dose expansion: Moderate to high membranous FRα expression in ≥50% of tumour cells, as assessed by immunohistochemistry (IHC) using an assay validated for this study.
    4. Tumour type eligibility:
    
       * Dose escalation: all solid tumours are eligible for escalation phase.
       * Dose expansion: restricted to PDAC and NPC only.
    5. Measurable disease per RECIST version 1.1.
    6. Mandatory baseline tumour biopsy, obtained prior to first administration of MC001, unless deemed unsafe by the investigator.
    7. ECOG performance status of 0 or 1.
    8. Adequate organ and marrow function, defined as:
    
       * Absolute neutrophil count (ANC) ≥1.5 × 10⁹/L
       * Platelet count ≥100 × 10⁹/L
       * Haemoglobin ≥9.0 g/dL
       * Creatinine clearance ≥50 mL/min (Cockcroft-Gault)
       * AST and ALT ≤3 × ULN (≤5 × ULN if liver metastases present)
       * Total bilirubin ≤1.5 × ULN
    9. Baseline imaging feasibility, defined as:
    
       * Ability to undergo contrast-enhanced CT at baseline, or
       * MRI abdomen/pelvis with non-contrast CT thorax if contrast CT is contraindicated.
    10. Recovered from prior therapy-related toxicities to:
    
        o Grade ≤1 per CTCAE v6.0
    11. Life expectancy ≥12 weeks, as assessed by the investigator.
    12. Women of childbearing potential (WOCBP) must:
    
        * Have a negative pregnancy test at screening.
        * Agree to use highly effective contraception during the study and for 6 months after the last dose of MC001.
        * Subjects not considered WOCBP are those without menses for ≥ 12 consecutive months, and those who have undergone hysterectomy and/or bilateral salpingo-oophorectomy. WOCBP must be willing to use acceptable methods of birth control (i.e., a hormonal contraceptive, intra-uterine device, diaphragm with spermicide, or condom with spermicide, or abstinence) for the duration of the study and 6 months after the last dose of treatment.
    13. Male participants with partners of childbearing potential are required to use barrier contraception in addition to having their partner use another method of contraception during the study and for 6 months after the last dose of treatment. Male participants will also be advised to abstain from sexual intercourse with pregnant or lactating women, or to use condoms.
    14. Ability to understand and voluntarily sign written informed consent.
    
    Exclusion Criteria:
    
    Participants meeting any of the following criteria will be excluded:
    
    1. Prior exposure to MMAE-containing antibody-drug conjugates, or known hypersensitivity to MMAE or related compounds.
    2. Peripheral neuropathy ≥ Grade 2 at baseline.
    3. Known active central nervous system (CNS) metastases or carcinomatous meningitis, unless:
    
       * Previously treated
       * Clinically stable for ≥4 weeks
       * Not requiring corticosteroids \>10 mg prednisolone (or equivalent) daily.
    4. Clinically significant ocular disease, including:
    
       * Active keratitis
       * Severe dry eye requiring ongoing ophthalmologic intervention
       * Corneal ulceration or other conditions that may increase ADC-related ocular risk.
    5. Uncontrolled intercurrent illness, including but not limited to:
    
       * Active infection requiring systemic therapy
       * Symptomatic congestive heart failure or LVEF \< 40% on screening echocardiogram
       * Unstable angina
       * Significant cardiac arrhythmia including QTc \> 500ms
       * Psychiatric illness or social situations limiting compliance.
    6. Receipt of any investigational study drug within 28 days or 5 half-lives (whichever is shorter) prior to the first administration of MC001.
    7. Major surgery within 4 weeks prior to first administration of MC001.
    8. Pregnant or breastfeeding women.
    9. Known uncontrolled viral infections, including:
    
       * Active hepatitis B or C with uncontrolled viral load
       * Known HIV infection with detectable viral load.
    
         \*Hepatitis B virus (HBV):
    
         • Participants who are HBsAg positive are eligible provided that:
       * They have received antiviral therapy for HBV for at least 4 weeks prior to the first dose of MC001, and they have an undetectable HBV viral load at screening.
       * Note: Participants must remain on HBV antiviral therapy throughout study treatment and follow local guidelines for continuation of antiviral therapy after completion of study treatment.
       * Patients who are HBsAg negative, and anti-HB core total positive are not considered to have active hepatitis if HB viral load is undetectable.
    
         • Hepatitis C virus (HCV):
       * Participants with a history of HCV infection are eligible provided that:
    
         1. HCV viral load is undetectable at screening, and
         2. Curative antiviral therapy was completed at least 12 weeks prior to initiation of study treatment.
    
            * Participants with active, uncontrolled viral hepatitis not meeting the above criteria will be excluded
    10. Second active malignancy requiring treatment.
    11. Any condition that, in the investigator's judgment, would place the participant at unacceptable risk or interfere with interpretation of study results.
